- Got to **Maintenance \> Equipment** menu.
- Open any equipment.
- You will see a Certification tab.

To configure expiration notifications:

- Go to **Settings \> Maintenance** and enable
  **Certificate Expiration Alert**.
- Go to **Maintenance \> Configuration \> Certificate Reminder Rules**
  to define how many days before expiration each reminder is sent.
- The daily cron job **Maintenance: Notify Expiring Certificates**
  will send email notifications to the assigned technician or owner.
